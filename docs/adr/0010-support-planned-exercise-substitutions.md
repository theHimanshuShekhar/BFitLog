# Support Exercise substitute links

BFitLog Exercises may link to an optional substitute Exercise. During workout logging, a User can record the substitute in place of the originally selected Exercise; the Workout Log must preserve both what was selected and what was actually performed so history, stats, and future progression logic remain understandable.

This supersedes the older wording where substitutions belonged to Planned Exercises inside a Training Day. Compatibility tables may remain during transition, but the product model is Exercise-to-Exercise substitution.
